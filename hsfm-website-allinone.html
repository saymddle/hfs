Our Story

Ignited by
His Spirit

Holy Spirit Fire Ministries is a Pentecostal Church with a vision to save souls for the Kingdom of God. We believe in the real and transforming power of the Holy Spirit — calling every person into a deep, living relationship with Jesus Christ.

Rooted in Scripture and led by the Spirit, our church family in Tucker, Georgia exists to worship boldly, teach faithfully, and serve our community and the nations with the love of Christ. Our doors are open to all who are seeking, searching, or simply curious.
