Who We Are

Ignited by
His Spirit

Holy Spirit Fire Ministries is a Pentecostal Church with a vision to save souls for the Kingdom of God. We believe in the real and transforming power of the Holy Spirit — calling every person into a deep, living relationship with Jesus Christ. Our doors are open to all.

What We Believe

Built on
three pillars

Our faith is rooted in Scripture, empowered by the Spirit, and expressed through service to our community and the world.

01 — Worship
Spirit & Truth

We encounter God through passionate, Spirit-led worship that transforms hearts and draws people into His presence. Every gathering is an invitation to go deeper.

02 — Word
Grounded in Scripture

The Word of God is our foundation. We preach the undiluted Gospel — teaching that equips believers to walk in authority, purpose, and power.

03 — Community
Called to Serve

We are a family called beyond ourselves. From Tucker to the nations, we carry the fire of revival wherever God sends us.
